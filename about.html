About North Star Bakery

Our Story

North Star Bakery began in 2013 as a small weekend market stand, created by a group of dedicated home bakers. Driven by a passion for bringing genuine, wholesome bread back to our community, we eventually opened our storefront on North Main St..

Sourcing & Milling Philosophy

Great bread begins with the soil. We source heritage varieties of grain from organic family farms direct from across the State. Our flours are milled fresh in small batches to preserve vital nutrients, deep grain flavors, and natural aroma – no chemical bleaching or preservatives.

Staff Highlights & Craft

Every morning at 3:00 AM our bakers come in and mix, shape, score and bake every batch of dough. Whether it’s caring for our heritage sourdough starter or laminating buttery croissants, each team member approaches their craft with a deep respect for the art of baking.
